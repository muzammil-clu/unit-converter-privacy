Privacy Policy — Unit Converter

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Unit Converter ("the App") is developed by Muzammil. This policy explains how the App handles user data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no internet connection required to use the App.

Local Storage

The App saves your recent unit-conversion history only on your own device using standard local app storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never sent to any server, developer, or third party
  • Can be cleared at any time from the History screen inside the App

Permissions

The App requests no special device permissions (no camera, location, contacts, microphone, or storage access beyond what the OS grants automatically to any app).

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics SDK, ad network, or tracking service.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
